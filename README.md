
# 🛍️ Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis (2022)

## 📌 Project Overview
Analyzed Vrinda Store's annual sales data for 2022 to understand customer behavior, sales trends, and business performance using Microsoft Excel.

## 🎯 Objective
Vrinda Store wants to create an annual sales report for 2022 to understand its customers and identify opportunities to grow sales in 2023.

## 📊 Dataset Overview
- **Dataset:** Vrinda Store Annual Sales 2022
- **Records:** 31,047 order records
- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **Data includes:** Customer details, order information, product categories, sales channels, order status, and transaction amounts.

## ❓ Business Questions
1. Compare sales and orders using a single chart.
2. Which month recorded the highest sales and orders?
3. Who purchased more: men or women?
4. What are the different order statuses?
5. Which are the top 5 states contributing to sales?
6. What is the relationship between age and gender based on orders?
7. Which sales channel contributes the most orders?
8. Which product category has the highest sales?

## ⚙️ Methodology
1. **Data Collection:** Imported the sales dataset into Excel.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Checked and handled missing or inconsistent data.
3. **Data Processing:** Organized and prepared data for analysis.
4. **Data Analysis:** Used Pivot Tables and Excel functions.
5. **Data Visualization:** Created charts to represent sales trends.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Built an interactive dashboard using Pivot Charts and Slicers.

## 📈 Key Insights
- March recorded the highest sales and orders.
- Women contributed approximately 64% of total sales.
- Approximately 92% of orders were successfully delivered.
- Adult women aged 30–49 contributed approximately 35% of orders.
- Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart accounted for approximately 80% of orders.
- Top 5 states contributing to sales:
  - Maharashtra
  - Karnataka
  - Uttar Pradesh
  - Telangana
  - Tamil Nadu

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Target women customers aged 30–49 through personalized marketing campaigns.
- Focus on high-performing states such as Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.
- Strengthen marketing strategies on Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart.
- Monitor monthly sales trends to improve promotional planning.
- Analyze returned and cancelled orders to improve customer experience.
